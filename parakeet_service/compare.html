Compare word times

Upload a clip, cut it to size, and add rows: a model, and optionally an aligner to re-time its words. Each row is one transcription on this server, run one at a time; a model or aligner loads on the first row that names it and stays loaded. Then click any word to hear exactly the span that row gave it.

Clip and rows

The file is decoded in your browser; only the clip between From and To is sent. A reference is exact word times, as JSON (words of a verbose_json response, or a list of {word, start, end}) or SRT/VTT with one cue per word, in the file's time.

    Timeline

    Grey is the waveform; each row below it is one row's words, and the shaded column is the selected word. Click a word, or focus the timeline and use the arrow keys: left and right step through words, up and down hear the same word from another row, Enter replays, Escape stops.
